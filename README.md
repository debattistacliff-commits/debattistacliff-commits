I'm an artist who loves experimenting with new things. I build apps to help people, and I don't believe useful tools should cost money. My goal is always the same: **take something powerful but complicated, and make it simple and easy to use.**

Many great apps only speak English, so I try to make mine available in **Maltese and other languages** too.

## 🛠 What I've made

### 🎨 [Froja Image Studio](https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/Froja-Image-Studio)
<a href="https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/Froja-Image-Studio"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/debattistacliff-commits/Froja-Image-Studio/HEAD/public/froja-brand-banner.png" width="100%" alt="Froja Image Studio"></a>

I love ComfyUI, but not everyone knows how to use it. Froja is a friendly, local image studio that sits on top of ComfyUI and Ollama, so anyone can create images with their own models and LoRAs without wiring up nodes.

### 🪐 [Orbital Launcher](https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/Orbital-Launcher)
<a href="https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/Orbital-Launcher"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/debattistacliff-commits/Orbital-Launcher/HEAD/docs/images/orbital-launcher-banner.png" width="100%" alt="Orbital Launcher"></a>

A frosted-glass ring launcher for Windows. Press **Alt + Space** for your apps, search, world radio, watch faces with live weather, and system controls.

### 👻 [Whispers of the Spirit](https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/whispers-of-the-spirit)
<a href="https://github.com/debattistacliff-commits/whispers-of-the-spirit"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/debattistacliff-commits/whispers-of-the-spirit/HEAD/assets/icon/whispers-of-the-spirit-512.png" width="120" alt="Whispers of the Spirit" align="right"></a>

A ghost-box and EVP radio console for Windows, Linux and Android. Made for ghost hunters in Malta and everywhere else, in six languages: 🇬🇧 English · 🇲🇹 Malti · 🇮🇹 Italiano · 🇪🇸 Español · 🇫🇷 Français · 🇩🇪 Deutsch.

## 💡 What I care about

- **Free for everyone.** Good tools shouldn't be locked behind a price.
- **Simple first.** If it needs a manual, it can be simpler.
- **Local and private.** Your models, your files, your computer.
- **Every language counts.** Including Maltese.
